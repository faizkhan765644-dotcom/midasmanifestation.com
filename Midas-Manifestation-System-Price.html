Midas Manifestation System Price: Full Breakdown

The headline price you see on the sales page is $37. The vendor describes this as a one-time payment with no monthly fees [citation:1]. That statement is technically true for the front-end product. However, it omits the post-purchase enrollment that most buyers overlook.

Cost Component Amount What You Need to Know
Main System (Front-End) $37 one-time This is the advertised price for the core audio program and PDF workbook [citation:1]. Covered by a 60-day refund policy through the payment processor [citation:2].
Midas Inner Circle Trial $37 / month ⚠️ CRITICAL: Checkout enrolls you in a 14-day trial. After 14 days, you are billed $37 monthly unless you cancel [citation:2]. This is not covered by the 60-day refund on the $37 front-end.
Optional Upsells Varies Additional training packages may be offered after checkout. These are separate purchases and not required.

Payment processor: Digistore24 or ClickBank (depending on the link). These are legitimate processors, which means your card details are secure — but also that the subscription terms are governed by the vendor, not the processor [citation:2].

⚠️ Real Cost Warning: If you buy the $37 product and forget to cancel the trial, you will pay $74 within 45 days ($37 front-end + $37 first month). Over six months, that becomes $259. The recurring charge is where the vendor makes its money. The $37 is a lead-in price [citation:2].

The Hidden Cost Most Reviewers Miss

This is the single most important section of this page. Multiple independent reviews have confirmed the same pattern: the $37 purchase is not the full financial commitment.

According to a detailed 2026 review, "at checkout, you're enrolled in a 14-day trial to the 'Midas Inner Circle' membership. After 14 days, you're charged $37/month unless you cancel" [citation:2]. The trial terms are buried in the checkout fine print, not shown in the main video sales letter.

The cancellation process is also not straightforward. You must contact the vendor's support desk directly. The payment processor (ClickBank or Digistore24) handles the initial $37 but cannot cancel the recurring subscription for you [citation:2]. If you request a refund on the $37, that does not automatically stop the monthly charges.
